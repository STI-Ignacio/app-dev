🧪 Breaking Bad
"I am the danger." — Walter White
📺 About the Series

Breaking Bad is a crime drama created by Vince Gilligan. It follows Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who starts making drugs after being diagnosed with cancer. As the story progresses, his journey takes a dark turn as he becomes more involved in the criminal world.

⭐ Why I Love It

I love Breaking Bad because of its amazing storytelling, intense plot twists, and incredible character development. Walter White's transformation is what makes the series so interesting and unforgettable.

🎬 Series Details
Genre: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Seasons: 5
Released: 2008–2013
Creator: Vince Gilligan
